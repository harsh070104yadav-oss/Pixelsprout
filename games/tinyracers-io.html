About TinyRacers.io

Tiny cars. Huge chaos. Take control of a remote-control buggy and tear across a giant world of toys and desk supplies, where pencils are speed bumps, rulers are ramps and every corner is a fight. TERRITORY RUSH – the .io mode Your buggy drags a pencil behind it. Leave your land, draw a loop and race back home to paint everything inside your color. Sounds easy? Every other driver is hunting your line. If they touch it before you get home, youre cut! Steal their land, swallow small rivals whole and knock them out of the round. Most land when the clock hits zero wins.
