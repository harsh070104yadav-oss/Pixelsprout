Tiny robots fight across oversized everyday spaces in RoboStrike 3D, a first-person arena shooter. Collect batteries to power your teams cannon and destroy the enemy tower, take control of a Mega Robot, or rack up eliminations in Deathmatch. Pick a class, customize your robot, and practice against bots or jump into an online match.
