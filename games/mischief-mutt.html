About Mischief Mutt

Mischief Mutt is a funny 3D open-town adventure where you play a cheeky dog with zero manners. Steal sausages from the butcher, knock over bins, scare pigeons, dig up flower beds and snatch steaks from the mansion BBQ. Every bit of mischief raises your wanted level - first the victim chases you, then the dog catcher van arrives with nets! Sprint, jump onto roofs and hide in bushes to escape. Recruit a gang of street dogs, take story jobs from Whiskers the gang-boss cat, and rank up from Puppy to Most Wanted to unlock new places: a beach boardwalk, a construction site with a giant crane, a farm with a grumpy farmer and the Dog Catcher HQ, where the final jailbreak awaits. Features: 23 story jobs plus endless odd jobs, daily challenges, 40 hidden golden bones, 17 trophies, 6 dog breeds, hats and upgrades. Progress is saved automatically.
