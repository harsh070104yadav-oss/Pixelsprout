About Animals Kart

Animals Kart is a fast-paced arcade racing game where cute animal drivers compete across colorful tracks in powerful little karts. Choose your favorite racer, master tight corners, use boosts, overtake your rivals and fight your way to the top of the World Championship. Race through tropical coasts, deserts, jungles and futuristic cities in a retro-inspired pseudo-3D racing experience designed for quick and exciting gameplay. Win championships, unlock new challenges and prove who is the fastest animal on the track! Race fast, drift through the corners and become the Animals Kart World Champion!
