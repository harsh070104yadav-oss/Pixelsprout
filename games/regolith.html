About REGOLITH

Your ship has crashed on an unknown planet and the only way out is down. Take your astronaut through five underground worlds and find the five missing parts of the ship. Everything in the cave is made of small pixels that behave like real stuff. Rock breaks and falls like sand, water puts out fire, lava turns to stone when it touches water, fuel burns and acid eats the ground. You can dig a tunnel anywhere, blow a hole in a wall with a mine or flood a room to get rid of the aliens inside.
