About Penguin Slugger

Grab the bat and send a very cute penguin flying! Set the angle, time the hit in the green zone, then fight for every meter: hold to glide, fire rockets, grab balloons, bounce off ice blocks and dodge snowmen. Collect fish, spend coins on a stronger bat, more rockets, a longer glide and bouncier landings, and unlock new penguin colors. The sky changes from night to sunset to aurora the farther you go. Game info: 5 upgrades with 10 levels each, 5 penguin colors, 5 languages.
