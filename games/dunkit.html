About DunkIt

Jump, flip and slam! DunkIt is an arcade basketball game with 35 physics-based dunk levels: trampolines, moving balls, obstacles and crazy trick dunks like the Tomahawk, Superman or Backwards Dunk. Time your grab and your slam perfectly to beat each level. Want more? Play quick matches against the AI: full-court 1v1 in 2D or half-court 3D games from 1v1 up to 3v3.
