About Moon Dust Simulator

Sweep the Moon clean! Start with a toothbrush, brush up colorful moon dust, pack it into capsules and deliver it to your ship for cash. Upgrade through 20 tools, from brooms and vacuums to rovers and a giant cleaning truck, and open 20 dust zones. Hire robot vacuums, build a pressure chamber, collect suits and pets, and clear hex sectors for bonus rewards. How clean can you get the Moon?
