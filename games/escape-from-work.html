How to start

Click OK, then PLAY NOW!, wait for the game to load (about 10 seconds in our session, with the bar stalling at 99%), then click Play. The page credits Erik Reis, and the game is served via GamePix.

How to play

WASD moves (we did not test A). The mouse looks around, with a white centre-dot crosshair. Click the game canvas if you need to recapture the mouse. In our session, E, the arrow keys, and clicking the floating blue work booklet in the aisle had no visible effect. That booklet looks like a Brazilian Carteira de Trabalho, but we had no confirmation that pickup worked.

Tips

  • Hold W and make small mouse moves; big swings slammed us into the shelves.
  • Stay mid-aisle; the shelf ends clipped the camera.
  • If you get wedged, hold S to back out.
  • Don't click sidebar ad areas.
