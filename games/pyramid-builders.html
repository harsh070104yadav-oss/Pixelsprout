About Pyramid Builders

How did they REALLY build the pyramids? Dig clay, stack bricks, hire a chunky crew... and dont ask about the UFO. Grab a shovel and start at the bottom. Dig clay at the pit, press it into bricks, let them dry on the rack and carry them up. Lay every brick of the Great Pyramid by hand, then hire help: diggers, porters, moulders, brick carriers and masons. Build mud huts for your crew, buy catapults that fling bricks onto the top, and blow the rally horn when you want them to hurry up. Then night falls, and something hums in the sky. Your new alien friends are very good at stacking. Sign the Alien Pact, and the work goes on all night. When a monument is done, the client comes to collect it, and the next order is a mystery. Was it a giant cat? A tower in a place called Paris? History will never know. - First-person building: dig, mould, carry and lay every brick yourself, or let your crew do it. - 5 kinds of workers, 16 upgrades and buildings, a brick trolley, catapults and a rally horn. - 7 monuments, then a new cycle at double pay. - Day and night cycle, random events, the Pharaohs chest and crew styles. - About 10 hours of content; progress saves automatically. - Plays on desktop, phone and tablet.
