About Tokyo Extreme Battleground

Pick one of 13 fighters and clash across seven Tokyo arenas, from neon alleys to a sunlit street lined with cherry blossoms. Rack up knockouts in Survival, face a rival in Duel, or sharpen your moves in Training. Use punches, sword strikes and special abilities, then unlock fighters and collect hats between matches.
