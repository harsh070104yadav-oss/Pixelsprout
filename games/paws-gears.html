About Paws Gears

Build crazy contraptions with springs, fans, balloons, seesaws, bumpers and portals to get the hungry cat to its fish! Paws & Gears is a physics puzzle game with 30 hand-made levels. Every level is a little machine waiting to be built. Drag the parts in, tilt them into place, press Go and watch the chain reaction. Learn a new tool every few levels, then combine them all in clever multi-step machines. Grab the 3 coins in each level for a perfect score, and spend your coins in the Wardrobe on new cats (Tuxedo, Calico, Siamese and more) and outfits like top hats, crowns, glasses and bow ties. Each new tool comes with a short Science bit explaining the real physics behind it.
