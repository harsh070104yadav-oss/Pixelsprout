About Road Trip Taxi Adventure

Taxi Road Rush: Pick & Drop is a 3D taxi driving game set on long forest and desert highways. Pick up passengers from petrol pumps, houses, dhabas and parks, then drop them at their destination before time runs out. Choose from 5 fast sports cars, each with its own speed, acceleration and handling. Dodge traffic, watch out for accident scenes on the road, and pass checkpoints to earn extra time. Every level gets harder, with more passengers and less time.
