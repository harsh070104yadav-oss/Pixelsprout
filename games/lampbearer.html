About Lampbearer

Single-player 3D action RPG. You are the last Lampbearer: three wardens went mad from the Blights whisper and their seals went dark. Your sword strikes on its own; you steer, dodge telegraphed blows and outlast the swarm. Lands: Drowned Ruins and the Bog Mothers mire, the Ash Smiths lava forge, the Dryad Queens jungle, and the Scar with the Heart of the Blight. Allies: Halvard the barbarian and Marin the water mage. Freed prisoners open camp shops with new moves, forged gear and spirit companions. Six bosses; turn bonfires, slag balls and flytraps against the horde.
