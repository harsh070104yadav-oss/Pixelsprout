About Blokoparkur

Blokoparkur is a first-person parkour game in a world made of blocks. Run, jump from block to block and reach the portal at the end of each level to unlock the next one. 20 levels take you from a sunny meadow and a forest stream to ice islands, fire ruins, a volcano forge and a crystal dawn. Every world has its own obstacles: moving platforms and lifts, crumbling blocks, slippery ice and slime that bounces you high into the air. Each level hides three diamonds - collect all 60. Checkpoints keep you from starting over, and the timer shows where you can be faster. Your best time on every level is saved, and the sum of your best times on the first 11 levels goes to the records table. Play on desktop or mobile: touch screens get a joystick and a jump button. The game is in English and Russian: the language follows the browser.
