About Granny House Escape

Play Granny House Escape free online in your browser on Pixelsprout — no download and no sign-up. It is a first-person horror game set in a dark house. Built for mouse and keyboard; touch play has not been tested on this page.

Last updated: 19 Nov 2026

Start by choosing Mouse & Keyboard on the controls screen, then Start Game, and Yes at the new-save prompt. WASD moves, and you turn the view by left-click-dragging; plain mouse movement does nothing. Pause is in the top left. E did nothing in our session.

The game opens in a very dark bedroom with a four-poster bed, a window with blinds, and a creepy portrait of Granny's face. Through the doorway there is a second door with a lit Exit sign. There is no on-screen objective. A publisher description for this title says you collect keys and solve puzzles; in our play session we did not find keys or puzzles, we got stuck at doorways, and we have not reached the exit yet.

Move slowly and use the wall lamps to find your way. Steer by click-dragging, and back off quietly if you see Granny. About five minutes in, Granny stood in the dark corridor under three wall lamps with her back turned and did not notice; we backed away, and there was no chase.
