About Carletto Dreams Adventure

Join Carletto on a magical journey through worlds hidden inside his dreams! Explore floating islands, collect glowing Dream Stars and use your telescope to reveal invisible bridges, secret paths and puzzle clues. Push crates, activate switches and dodge mischievous creatures as you explore 16 levels, including a final boss challenge. Discover an illustrated story filled with wonder, surprises and adventure - then wake up ready for another day!
