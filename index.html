Yes. Every game on Pixelsprout is free to play. The site is supported by ads.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Games are HTML5 and play directly in your browser tab. There is nothing to install.

Do I need an account?

No. You don't need a Pixelsprout account to play.

Games are HTML5 and run in the Chrome browser, so they usually work on Chromebooks if your network allows the site. We have not tested every game on every model, and we do not help bypass school or work filters.

Games are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens.
