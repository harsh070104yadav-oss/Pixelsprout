How Sprout XP works

  • A game session counts once you have actively played it for 60 seconds with the page visible. Opening or refreshing a page earns nothing.
  • Your first counted session earns +10 XP. Coming back and playing on a later day earns +25 XP once per day. Playing on 3 different days in the same week earns a +75 XP weekly bonus.
  • Quests reward exploring: new games, new categories and active days. Each quest pays out once per day or week.
  • Days reset at 00:00 UTC. Weeks and the Sprout League reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. Lifetime XP and your level never reset.
  • There is a daily XP limit, and XP is calculated on our server, so repeated reloads or scripts do not help. There are no cash prizes and nothing to buy.
  • Missing a day never costs you anything. There are no streaks to lose.

Your anonymous player ID

Pixelsprout never asks for a name, email or phone number. The first time you use these features, this browser gets a random ID stored in a first-party cookie (ps_pid). We keep your XP, quests, favourites and recently played games against that ID only. We do not use fingerprinting and we never send this ID to Google Analytics or advertisers.

If you clear cookies, use a private window, switch browser or use another device, you will get a new ID and start fresh. We cannot tell that it is you, and we do not try to.
