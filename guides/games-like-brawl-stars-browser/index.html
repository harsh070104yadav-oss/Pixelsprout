Games Like Brawl Stars You Can Play in Your Browser

Advertisement

Can you play Brawl Stars in a browser? Not the official game. Pixelsprout does not host Supercell's Brawl Stars, and nothing on this page is made by or affiliated with Supercell. What you can play free in your browser are arena and .io games with a similar feel: quick matches, heroes or characters that power up, and lots of dodging and shooting. Here are the best ones on Pixelsprout.

About the "Brawl Stars" games on Pixelsprout

Pixelsprout lists two browser games from third-party publishers whose titles use the name: Brawl Stars and Brawl Stars 2. These are not Supercell's Brawl Stars. They are unofficial .io-style arena games. You pick a hero, eat food items to evolve and grow stronger, and battle other players. If you want the real Brawl Stars, download it from the official app store on your phone. If you want a quick arena game in a browser tab, read on.

Browser arena games with a Brawl Stars feel

  • Brawlers Arena Battle Stars: arena skirmishes where you pick weapons, survive waves of enemies and upgrade your gear.
  • Ace Brawl Battle 3D: a 3D arena shooter. Keep moving, use power-ups and outplay your opponents.
  • Archers.io: start as one archer, recruit allies into an army and fight rival teams.
  • Guardz IO: a medieval knight .io game with sword combat. You rank up from Page to King's Champion.
  • Sword Battle.io: multiplayer sword duels where you collect coins to get stronger.
  • Diep.io: a classic tank arena. Upgrade your basic tank into stronger classes as you score.
  • BattleGrid: fast PvP tank battles where you move, fire and unlock abilities.

Play with a friend on one keyboard

If what you like about Brawl Stars is playing with friends, try local two-player games like Basketball Stars or Fireboy and Watergirl Online, or browse two-player games.

Tips for browser arena and .io games

  1. Keep moving. Standing still is the fastest way to lose in any arena game.
  2. Grow before you fight. In .io games, collect food, orbs or coins early and avoid bigger players until you have levelled up.
  3. Use cover and edges. Walls and map edges limit where enemies can come from.
  4. Pick one upgrade path. Putting all your points into a few stats usually beats spreading them out.
  5. Expect other players. Some .io games match you with other online players, so results vary from match to match.

What do people like about Brawl Stars?

Brawl Stars is popular because matches are short, the controls are simple and there are lots of characters with different abilities. You can jump in for three minutes and still feel like you made progress. Browser arena and .io games share some of that: you start a match right away, power up during the round, and try again straight after you lose. What they usually do not have is a large roster of polished heroes, ranked modes or the progression systems of a big mobile game. Think of them as quick, free arena fun in a browser tab rather than a replacement for the official app. If you enjoy one of them, check its game page for controls and tips before your next match.
