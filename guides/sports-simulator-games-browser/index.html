Sports Simulator Games in Your Browser — No Download

You can play sports simulator games in your browser on Pixelsprout, with no download and no account. That includes Referee Simulator 3D, where you take the whistle in a 3D match, plus arcade soccer, street cricket and other short sports titles. Open a page below and play in the tab.

Sports games you can start right now

These are HTML5 games, not a licensed league sim and not a claim that they match a real-world broadcast. Games are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens.

  • Referee Simulator 3D — Run with play, call fouls, penalties, offsides and cards, and use a pitchside VAR monitor when you are unsure.
  • Gully Cricket — Street-style cricket in tight spaces, with batting and bowling in short matches.
  • Crazy Goal — Arcade soccer across many short levels; the finishing is the challenge.
  • Ninja Head Ball — Head-ball arcade matches with quick dodges and strikes.
  • Billiards — 3D pool, billiards and snooker from the creator tailuge, embedded from the creator's site.

Referee Simulator 3D in a little more detail

Instead of taking a shot, you officiate. Stay close to the play, decide whether a tackle is clean, and use the VAR screen when a call is close. It is a good neighbour to the other sports games if you want something that is still about a match but not another goal-scorer. The game is free to open in the browser; Pixelsprout does not add its own career mode or prizes on top.

For more sports titles, see the sports category, browser games for Chromebook.

Tips

In Referee Simulator 3D, keep the play in view before you reach for a card, and use VAR when you are genuinely unsure rather than on every whistle. In Gully Cricket, wait for the ball rather than swinging at everything. In Crazy Goal, learn one level's layout, then go for the finish. In Billiards, use a lighter shot to set up the next ball when a direct pot is thin.
