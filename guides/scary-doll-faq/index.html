FAQ

Can I play Scary Doll free online with no download?

Yes. The game loads in the browser tab on Pixelsprout. Nothing to install and no account required.

What is Scary Doll?

A first-person horror game. The game-info screen says a child using black magic scares the town and hides in a house, and that you must destroy his powers.

How do I start Scary Doll?

Click OK, then PLAY NOW, then PLAY, then SELECTION LEVEL (only level 1 was unlocked in our session), then GAME INFO, then Continue.

What does the game-info screen say?

A child using black magic scares the town and hides in a house, and you must destroy his powers.

What are the objectives?

The first objective text is "THIS HOME IS HAUNTED. IF YOU HAVE GUTS THEN YOU CAN GO IN IT". The next is "GRAB THIS KEY AND OPEN THIS DOOR, HIDE FROM CHILD".

How do I control Scary Doll?

The game shows "WASD to move"; you can also use the joystick at the bottom left. Drag the mouse to turn the view a little. The objective OK button often needs two clicks. Built for mouse and keyboard; touch play has not been tested on this page.

What is on the HUD?

A compass with a red dot for the child, a camera inset at the top that shows him, pause and skip buttons, and a lightbulb hint icon.

How do I find my way?

Glowing footprints lead to the house, and a blue diamond marks the key.

What happens if you get caught?

It is pitch dark inside the house. In our session we were caught with no warning: the screen showed blood and three red claw marks, then GAME OVER, with Restart and Main Menu.

What tips help at the start?

Watch the compass dot and the camera inset, follow the footprints and the blue diamond, and click OK twice if an objective does not close.

Where did you get stuck?

Inside the dark house. We did not find the key or any hiding spots.

What similar games can I play next?
