Free Casual Games Online, No Download: Falling Dummy and Escape Tsunami

Yes. You can play free casual games online on Pixelsprout with no download and no account. Two we have actually played are Falling Dummy, a ragdoll launch where you send a dummy through a fall, and Escape Tsunami For Brainrots, a short chase where you try to outrun labelled tsunami waves.

The notes below come from recorded play sessions. For the rest of the catalog, browse the casual games hub.

Falling Dummy

Level 1 starts with a power meter and a shopping-cart ramp. Mid-air steering keys were not logged, so we are not inventing them here. After a fall, a damage counter (a left bar or oval) is visible, and sometimes a distance in metres. Completing a level shows a COMPLETED screen, sometimes with a Reward figure. On-screen lines we have actually seen include "Keep Crushing!", COMPLETED, and a damage score.

The X-ray bone-break imagery is graphic. This is Casual on the site, not Kids.

First minute

After the game loaded, Level 1 booted with the power meter and the shopping-cart ramp. The cart got a rocket, went through the structure, a bone X-ray hit, and a score of 842 was visible. COMPLETED followed, with a skeleton, confetti, and Reward 101. Level 2 then loaded.

Tips from play

  • Read the damage number off the counter at the end of the fall, not mid-fall. Mid-fall values can be misleading.
  • A landing does not always mean the counter stops. On one Level 4 glitch, the dummy stood still on a ledge at 132m while damage kept climbing, from 96 to 1,250, then 3,026, and finally 4,883.
  • Levels 1 to 6 in our later recordings each showed COMPLETED after the fall. That is a finish screen plus the damage total, not a high score you have to beat.
  • Later levels exist: we reached Levels 7 to 10, and Level 11 froze on a rooftop at 1450. We do not have a fuller sheet of damage totals for Levels 7 to 10.

Other final damage totals we can point to: 852 and 2,420 on two early falls, then 2,136 on Level 4, 3,890 on Level 5, and 2,483 on Level 6. We are not quoting other reward amounts from later clips.

Escape Tsunami For Brainrots

In the footage the player runs, including into a corridor. Exact keys were not logged, and mouse look was not separately noted, so we are not inventing WASD or other bindings. The goal is to outrun or dodge labelled tsunami waves. We saw no separate tutorial text in the timeline.

First minute

The first minute of gameplay was not broken out in detail in our notes. Across the 8.5-minute play window, the HUD showed Record: 13m and $0, plus wave-speed labels Slow, Medium, Very Slow, and Fast. Treat "Record: 13m" as the number on the HUD, not as a claim that this session itself ran for 13 minutes.

Tips from play

  • The speed label on the wave matters, and even Very Slow flattened us.
  • Running into a corridor is not a guaranteed save. A blue wave hit us there, we went underwater, and then we respawned.
  • Fast waves can hit more than once in a short span. We were engulfed by Fast twice close together.
  • Quote only HUD text you can point to. We had Record: 13m and $0 on the main session, and a later clip showed Record: 7m while running for a corridor, with the wave arriving first.

This one is easy to understand and easy to get flattened. It suits short "can you outrun it?" sessions.
