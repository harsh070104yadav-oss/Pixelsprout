Sites Like Poki and CrazyGames (Free, No Download)

Advertisement

Looking for sites like Poki or CrazyGames? Short answer: Coolmath Games, itch.io, Newgrounds, Armor Games and Pixelsprout are the main free alternatives. Each one plays in a normal web browser, but they suit different players. This guide explains what each site is good at, where it falls short, and where Pixelsprout fits, including its weak spots.

What makes a good Poki or CrazyGames alternative?

Most people asking for a Poki or CrazyGames alternative want the same few things: games that load in the browser tab, no installer, no forced sign-up, and enough variety that you are not stuck with the same ten titles. Some also want a site that is less crowded, a different mix of genres, or games that work on a Chromebook. Keep those needs in mind when you pick from the list below.

The main alternatives, compared honestly

Coolmath Games

Best for logic, puzzle and strategy games with a family-friendly feel. It is well known in schools. The catalogue leans toward thinking games rather than shooters or racing.

itch.io

Best for indie and experimental games. Many games are free and playable in the browser, but itch.io is also a store, so some titles are paid or download-only. Quality varies a lot because anyone can upload.

Newgrounds

Best for creator-made games and animation with a long history. It has an active community and ratings, and the content ranges from very polished to rough experiments.

Armor Games

Best for classic portal-style games, including many strategy, puzzle and idle titles that grew out of the Flash era.

Pixelsprout

Pixelsprout is a free browser game portal with more than 5,000 HTML5 games. You don't need an account and there is nothing to download. Games cover puzzle, arcade, action, racing, sports, two-player, horror and many more genres. Most games come from third-party HTML5 publisher networks, and a few, listed in the Originals section, are Pixelsprout originals. The site is supported by ads.

Where Pixelsprout is weaker: it is a newer site with a smaller community than Poki or CrazyGames. It has no player accounts, profiles or cross-device saves. Games are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens.

Tips for picking a browser game site

  1. Check what you will actually play. A huge catalogue matters less than having good games in the two or three genres you like.
  2. Test on your device. Many portal games are made for desktop. If you are on a phone or tablet, try a couple of games before settling on a site.
  3. Look at sign-up rules. If you do not want an account, choose sites where play does not require one. Pixelsprout, Poki and CrazyGames all let you play without signing up.
  4. Expect ads on free sites. Free portals are usually paid for by ads. That is normal; just avoid sites that push downloads or browser extensions.
  5. Respect school and work rules. If a network blocks a site, that is the network owner's decision. We do not help anyone get around filters.
