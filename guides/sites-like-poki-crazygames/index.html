Tips for picking a browser game site

  1. Check what you will actually play. A large catalogue matters less than having games in the genres you like.
  2. Test on your device. Many portal games are made for desktop. If you are on a phone or tablet, try a couple of games before settling on a site.
  3. Expect ads on free sites. Free portals are usually paid for by ads. Avoid sites that push downloads or browser extensions you did not ask for.
  4. Respect school and work rules. If a network blocks a site, that is the network owner's decision. We do not help anyone get around filters.
