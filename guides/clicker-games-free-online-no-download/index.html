Free Clicker Games Online, No Download: Factory, Soccer and Dog Clicker

Yes. You can play free clicker games online on Pixelsprout with no download and no account. Three we have actually played are Factory Clicker, a resource clicker with upgrades, Soccer Clicker Game, a 35-second kick challenge, and Dog Clicker, a puppy clicker with a shop on the right.

The notes below come from a live play session on 25 November 2026. For the rest of the catalog, browse the clicker games hub.

Factory Clicker

The page loads in about 15 seconds: a site "OK, PLAY NOW!" overlay, a Construct 3 splash, then the game's yellow Play button. There were no ads over the game.

Click the big resource panels: Energy, Wood, Stone, Metal and Plastic. A top bar shows your resources. Home, 1 through 5, and Next switch screens, and there are Save and Load buttons. The goal is to gather resources and buy "+1 Click Strength" and per-resource "Auto-Click" upgrades. Later screens add more resources.

First minutes

Energy went from 0 to 202 after about 20 seconds, at about 1.5 per click, then to 914, then 1,101. We bought +1 Click Strength at 1,101, which left 101, and the next cost was 2,000. We bought Energy Auto-Click for 1,000, and afterwards it added about 1 Energy per second.

Tips from play

  • Put everything into Energy first, since every upgrade costs 1,000 or more.
  • The Energy Auto-Click pays off fastest.
  • Expect about 2 minutes of steady clicking before the first upgrade.

Soccer Clicker Game

This one loads fine. The play area is portrait, with black bars around it even in fullscreen. There were no ads over the game. Click the field to start, then click as fast as possible to kick. There is no upgrade shop. The goal is the most kicks before the 35-second timer ends.

Real numbers

Round 1 was 460 kicks. Round 2 was 524 kicks, a new best.

Tips from play

  • Click "CLICK HERE 2 PLAY" to restart.
  • Any click on the field counts, so stay in one spot.
  • Scores improve with repeat rounds.

Dog Clicker

This is a Unity game and loaded in about 15 seconds. In fullscreen it filled only the top half of the screen, with a blue area below, but it stayed fully playable. Click the puppy in the centre; mini puppies spawn as you click. Upgrades sit on the right: +1 for 100, +2 for 2,000, +5 for 10,000, +10 for 20,000, and +25 for 70,000.

Real numbers

Clicks went from 0 to 267 in about 22 seconds. We bought Upgrade +1 and had 167 left, climbed to 2,243, bought Upgrade +2 and had 243 left, and ended at 931.

Tips from play

  • Buy Upgrade +1 the moment you reach 100.
  • Affordable upgrade buttons turn bright white.
  • Click the big dog, not the mini puppies.
