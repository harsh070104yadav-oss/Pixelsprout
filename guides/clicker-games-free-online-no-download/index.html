How these clickers work

The loop is simple: click to earn, buy an upgrade, click less for the same result, then buy the next upgrade. Eggcavation dresses that loop up as a first-person digging job — you fill a bucket, sell shards at a terminal, and spend the cash on a pickaxe, a jackhammer or a drill. Candy Clicker 2 and Cat Clicker stay closer to a classic button-and-upgrade screen.

Pixelsprout does not keep a cloud save for you. If a game offers its own save in the browser, that is local to that title. We also do not invent player counts or ranks for these games.

Tips that apply to most clickers

Buy the cheapest upgrade that clearly raises your income, then check whether a bigger tool is worth the wait. If the game has an auto-clicker or a drone, get that before luxury cosmetics. In Eggcavation, empty the bucket at the terminal often enough that you are not standing around with a full load. When you return to a tab after a break, look at the in-game messages before you click wildly — some titles pause until you press continue.
