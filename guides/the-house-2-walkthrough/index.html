Play it here: The House 2.

How we got inside

Our first clicks on the boarded front door of The House 2 did absolutely nothing, and then, a few seconds later, the door creaked open by itself. That's this game in one sentence: slow, quiet and built to make you doubt yourself. Here's how to get inside and what we found in the first room.

What The House 2 is

A short point-and-click horror game from Sinthai Studio, rated PG-13 on screen. The story: a wealthy family (father, mother, daughter) and their maid all died in the house, the neighbors sealed it, and people say they hear a woman screaming inside. You go in anyway. It plays free in your browser on Pixelsprout, no download.

How to start

  1. Open the game page and click "OK, PLAY NOW!", then the orange "Play" button once it loads (about 10 to 15 seconds).
  2. The intro story plays. Click the small "SKIP INTRO" at the bottom of the title screen if you've seen it.
  3. On the outside of the house, click the red "GET INSIDE" label by the porch.
  4. At the boarded front door, click the door and wait. It responds slowly, and in our run it opened after a few clicks (a double-click did it).
  5. A chapter card shows the five rooms: Living Room, Bathroom, Bedroom, Working Room and Safe Room. Only the Living Room is open at the start; the others stay grayed out until you progress. This page does not include steps for those later rooms.

Controls

  • Mouse only. Click things to interact.
  • A hand cursor means a hotspot. Scenery keeps the normal arrow.
  • We saw no inventory in the first room.
  • Use the page's Fullscreen button to make the game frame bigger. Esc exits fullscreen.

What's in the Living Room

  • A note on the armchair, dated September 19, 1964, from the nursemaid: she can feel "something strange" in the house and wonders if she should quit. Click it to read, click again to close.
  • A framed black-and-white family photo on the cabinet: mother, young girl and father.
  • A light switch left of the cabinet (it shows a hand cursor).
  • A doorway to a bathroom on the left, a dark staircase on the right, a wall vent and a radiator.

Tips

  1. Be patient. The game can take 2 to 4 seconds to react, so click once and wait before clicking again.
  2. Read every note and photo. They're the story, and they're how the house tells you who lived here.
  3. Check the hand cursor over everything, including small objects on furniture.
  4. Some hotspots don't do anything yet. In our run the light switch showed a hand cursor but nothing visible changed, so the next step may depend on something else in the room.
  5. Play with sound on. The ambient audio is most of the tension.

We didn't get past the Living Room in our session, so we're not going to make up the rest. If you've found the way out, tell us what worked.
