Bottle Flip Game and close variants

The skill is timing: too soft and the bottle does not rotate; too hard and it over-spins or flies off the landing spot. Open the game and follow the control prompt in the frame (usually a click or a short hold). Games are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens.

  • Bottle Flip Game — Flip a bottle through a series of rooms and try to land it on each target.
  • BottleFlip — Another bottle-flip title on the same site if you want a second layout.
  • Jump Bottle — A close physics cousin: jump and land a bottle rather than only flipping in place.

Tips for a clean landing

Watch one full hop before you commit, so you know how strong the flip is. Aim for the center of the landing surface, not the edge. If the bottle keeps falling forward, use a slightly softer flip; if it falls back toward you, add a little more. After a new room appears, look for a low ceiling or a moving object before you jump.
