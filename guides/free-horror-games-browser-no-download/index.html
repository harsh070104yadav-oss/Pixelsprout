Free Horror Games You Can Play in Your Browser

Advertisement

Looking for free horror games to play in your browser with no download? Pixelsprout's horror section has escape-the-house games, stealth games where you hide from a monster, creepy point-and-click mysteries and a few horror shooters. They all load in a browser tab and you don't need an account. Here is how they differ, so you can pick the right level of scary.

Escape-the-house and stealth horror

These games are about sneaking, finding keys and getting out before something catches you.

  • Granny House Escape: sneak around a creepy house, collect keys and solve puzzles to escape the granny.
  • Scary Doll: you are trapped in a decaying house with a porcelain doll. Make too much noise and she comes for you.
  • Scary House Clown Evil: a 3D haunted mansion where you avoid an evil clown.
  • Dr. Psycho - Hospital Escape: escape an empty hospital with no weapons, only stealth.
  • Escape From Work: an office maze where you avoid the boss and the cameras. Less gore, more tension.

Creepy mysteries and point-and-click

Slower games that build atmosphere instead of chasing you.

  • The House 2: explore a haunted house and click for clues about a family's dark past.
  • Exhibit of Sorrows: a point-and-click adventure through an unsettling circus exhibit.
  • Boo Scared? Escape from Backrooms: solve riddles to escape a maze before a cat catches you. It's more silly-scary than terrifying.

Horror action and shooters

  • The Malevolent Mansion of Evil: a first-person action horror game in a gothic manor.
  • Horror Sniper Clown Ghost: a first-person sniper game in a nightmare forest.
  • Floppa Scary Horror: a meme horror game where you collect eight cat photos in a dark forest while avoiding Big Floppa. It has jump scares.

Tips for browser horror games

  1. Use headphones. Many stealth horror games use sound cues, like footsteps or doors, to warn you.
  2. Move slowly in stealth games. Running is often louder and draws the monster to you.
  3. Learn the map before you rush. In escape games, remember where the doors, keys and hiding spots are.
  4. Play in full screen. It helps with immersion and makes small clues easier to see.
  5. Choose your scare level. Start with a point-and-click mystery or Escape From Work if you want tension without heavy jump scares.

Are these games suitable for kids?

Some of these games have jump scares, creepy images or violence. They are aimed at teens and adults, not young children. For younger players, try kids games or puzzle games instead.

What makes a good browser horror game?

The best browser horror games work with simple tools: sound, darkness, limited vision and a threat you cannot fight. Escape games give you a clear goal, like finding the keys and getting out, and the fear comes from not knowing where the monster is. Point-and-click mysteries are slower and use atmosphere, clues and story. Horror shooters give you a weapon, which makes them more action than fear. Because these games run in a browser tab, they are usually shorter than big console horror games, often a single session. That makes them a good fit for a quick scare with friends or a Halloween night. Turn the lights down, put headphones on and play in full screen for the best effect.

For stealth and escape, start with Granny House Escape or Scary Doll. For something less intense, try The House 2 or Escape From Work.

Some do, especially Floppa Scary Horror and the chase-style escape games. Point-and-click mysteries like The House 2 rely more on atmosphere.
