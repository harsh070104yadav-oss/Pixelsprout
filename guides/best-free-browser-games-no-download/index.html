Rage and climbing

These games reward careful movement. Progress is slow; one slip can undo a stretch of work.

Getting Over It — Guide a cat in a flower pot up a pile of giant Scratch blocks with a hammer. This is a fan-made Scratch remake by griffpatch, not the original Bennett Foddy game. Hold New Game briefly so the run actually starts. Built for mouse play.

Carrot Climber — Jump a rabbit from hook to hook up a wall to collect carrots. Miss a hook and you fall.

Going up — Tap to fly a bird through obstacles and collect coins.

Buba Jump — A one-button jumper where timing matters as the platforms get smaller.

Canjump — One-button jumps as a monster through tougher obstacles.

Physics, stunts and crashes

Moto X3M: Spooky Land — Motorbike stunt tracks with spooky obstacles. This is the Spooky Land page we host, not a claim that we have every Moto X3M pack.

Drive Mad 2 — 3D driving on strange obstacle tracks, with nitro and drifting.

Books Tower — Click to drop each sliding book onto the stack and see how tall a tower you can build.

Falling Dummy — Steer a dummy as it falls and hits things on the way down.

Car Crash Test — A crash-test physics game on this site.

Bottle Flip Game — Flip a bottle so it lands on shelves, tables and other furniture.

Body Drop 3D — Aim a limited number of balls at a ragdoll dummy.

Faily Skater — A 3D skating runner with tricks and obstacles.

Puzzle and short breaks

2048 — Slide tiles on a grid and merge matching numbers.

Stackblocks — A falling-block puzzle on the Stackblocks page (this is not listed here as Tetris).

Snake — A Pixelsprout original: steer the snake, eat, and avoid your tail.

Test Tubes. Water Sort — Pour colours between tubes until each tube holds one colour.

Sports and quick matches

Basketball Stars — One-on-one or two-on-two basketball, including local two-player on one keyboard.

Protect My Dog — A short action game about keeping a dog safe.
