Your Dream Room and other design games

Your Dream Room is a room-design game: you furnish empty spaces until they feel finished. Amanda's Perfect Date is more of a dress-up and date-prep game, which is a close neighbour if you like choosing outfits and interiors. Games are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens.

  • Your Dream Room — Choose wallpaper, flooring, furniture and accessories for a room.
  • Girl Game Organizing Fun — Sticker puzzles, cozy room organizing and cleaning challenges.
  • Big Supermarket Simulator — Run and tidy a small shop, then upgrade it with the money you earn.
  • Amandas Perfect Date — Dress Amanda, pick a look and a date, and see how the evening goes.

What to expect

These are calm, click-to-choose games rather than action titles. You usually browse a catalog of items, place or swap them, and move on to the next room or task. There is no downloadable decorator app and no sign-up wall on Pixelsprout. Some games may show their own in-game shop; that belongs to the game, not to a Pixelsprout account.

If you want a mix of short casual games as well as decoration, see free online puzzle games, browser games for Chromebook.

A few practical tips

If a room has a budget or a limited set of pieces, place the large furniture first and fill gaps after. In organizing games, sort one surface at a time so you can see what is left. In Amanda's Perfect Date, look at the on-screen choices before you confirm an outfit — you can usually back up a step if the game offers a redo.
