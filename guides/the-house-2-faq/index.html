What is The House 2?

A short point-and-click horror game from Sinthai Studio, rated PG-13 on screen. You search rooms in a haunted house and piece together a dark family history.

How do you play The House 2?

Click around the rooms to examine objects and pick up clues. In our play session the controls were mouse only: a hand cursor marks a hotspot. Use the page's Fullscreen button to make the game frame bigger; Esc exits fullscreen. Touch play has not been tested on this page.

How do I get past the boarded front door?

Double-click the middle of the door, not the handle, and wait 3–4 seconds; it took 3–4 tries before the planks dropped and the door creaked open.

What are the chapters/rooms?

The tabs are Living Room, Bathroom, Bedroom, Working Room and Safe Room, and the first chapter is the Living Room.

What small details are worth checking?

The note on the armchair is from the nursemaid, dated September 19, 1964, and the family photo on the cabinet zooms in on a double-click. The game shows a PG-13 warning at the start.
