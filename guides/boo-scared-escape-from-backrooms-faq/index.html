How do you play Boo Scared? Escape from Backrooms?

It is first-person 3D. W and S move forward and back, A strafes left (we did not test D), and the mouse looks around. E interacts; a hand icon appears on furniture. Click and Space did nothing in our session. Built for mouse and keyboard; touch play has not been tested on this page.

What is the stamina bar?

There is a stamina bar in the bottom right of the screen.

What is the start area like?

The game starts in a bright yellow Backrooms hallway with fluorescent lights, then a closed olive-green room. In that room we saw a blood-stained table, an overturned chair, two beds, a wardrobe, a bookshelf, and a framed portrait of a pale cat wearing a necklace. Players can get stuck in the closed room; we found no exit or puzzle trigger there.

Are the R and V ad boosts required?

No. The R and V prompts are optional watch-an-ad boosts: R is 1.5x move speed for 2 minutes, and V is invisibility for 1 minute. You do not need them to play.
