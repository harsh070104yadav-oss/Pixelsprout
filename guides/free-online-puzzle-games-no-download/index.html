Free Puzzle Games Online, No Download: Protect My Dog and Bottle Flip

Advertisement

Yes. You can play free puzzle games online on Pixelsprout with no download and no account. Two we played are Protect My Dog, a draw-a-line puzzle where you shield a dog from bees, and Bottle Flip Game, a timing puzzle where you flip a bottle so it lands upright.

The notes below come from recorded play sessions, not publisher blurbs. For the rest of the catalog, browse the puzzle games hub.

Protect My Dog

Draw one line by holding the mouse and dragging. An ink bar limits how long that line can be. The line is a physics object: it can be pushed, roll, or fall. The goal is to shield the dog, or dogs, from bees until the countdown ends. On the levels we timed, that timer was 10 seconds.

Desktop-mouse drags sometimes fail to register a line. If a drag draws nothing, try again in the game canvas, not the sidebar. We have had sessions where later levels drew no line at all.

First minute

Level 1 is a single dog. A dome with a flat base on the ground survived and showed LEVEL CLEARED and +20 coins. One later session opened on Level 3, with two dogs. The first three attempts drew no visible line and failed, and a shallow arch and an upside-down U also failed. A closed loop around both dogs cleared with 3 stars, and the coin balance was 50.

Tips from play

  • Close the shape fully. In one session, both Level 6 fails we logged were bees getting through a small gap.
  • Anchor the line to the ground or a wall. A loose ring can be pushed off, and the dog falls with it.
  • Plan before you draw. Long shapes run out of ink before they close.
  • We did not beat Level 6 in any session we logged. On one Level 6 attempt, bees pushed the ring off the spikes and the dog dropped into the lava with 7 seconds left.

On-screen outcomes we saw were LEVEL CLEARED and LEVEL FAILED! "Get x2" is an ad; we never clicked it. Levels 1 to 3 are quick. Level 6 is a real wall.

Bottle Flip Game

The splash text we saw is "TAP TO FLIP…". Timing the click or tap is the skill. We did not log exact key bindings beyond that. The goal is to flip the bottle so it lands upright on the target: first a lamp, then a table in later rounds. The play page slug is bottle-flip-game.

First minute

After the splash, we landed a clean upright flip on the red lamp, then missed the next flip. The fail text on screen is spelled OPPS!, along with LEVEL FAILED. A later session opened with ads, which are not part of the puzzle, and then moved to the table, not the lamp. In a third session we stayed on the lamp and did not reach a harder stage.

Tips from play

  • A clean lamp land does not mean the next flip will also land. Ours went in, then failed right after.
  • On the table, landing on the table is not the same as staying there. In one session, try 5 hit the table and still fell off.
  • Do not treat the chair as a reliable landing. In most rounds of that table session, the bottle bounced off it.
  • Ads also appeared mid-session. They are not part of the puzzle.

We had no readable score HUD on the lamp or fail beats. In the table session we logged 6 rounds, and 0 stayed on the table. The lamp is the friendly start; the table is the frustrating one.

FAQ

Are these puzzle games free, and do I need to download anything?

What is a good free puzzle game to start with?

Start with Protect My Dog on Level 1, or with the lamp stage of Bottle Flip Game. Both open quickly. Protect My Dog Level 6 and the Bottle Flip table are much harder.

How do you play Protect My Dog?

Hold the mouse and drag to draw one line that shields the dog from bees until the countdown ends. Close the shape and anchor it to the ground or a wall. An ink bar limits how long the line can be. On the levels we timed, the bee timer was 10 seconds.

Did you land Bottle Flip Game on the table?

No. In the table session we logged, 6 rounds ended with 0 stays. Try 5 hit the table and still fell off. The first target we landed was a red lamp; the next flip after that still failed, with on-screen text LEVEL FAILED and OPPS!

Do these puzzle games work on phones?

They are built for mouse and keyboard on desktop and have not been tested on touch screens. Bottle Flip Game's splash says TAP TO FLIP, but that is on-screen text, not a phone test.
