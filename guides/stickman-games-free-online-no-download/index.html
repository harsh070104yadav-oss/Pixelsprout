Tips

In timing levels, watch a full cycle of a moving platform before you jump. In hook games, release at the top of the swing, not at the bottom. In draw-to-save levels, short strokes are easier to place than a long scribble. In the sandbox, upgrade a tool you already understand before you switch style.
