Free Arcade Games Online, No Download: Buba Jump and Car Crash Test

Advertisement

Yes. You can play free arcade games online on Pixelsprout with no download and no account. Two we have actually played are Buba Jump, a portrait climbing run that tracks height as a score, and Car Crash Test, a crash-physics sandbox.

The notes below come from recorded play sessions. We did not write down exact keys or taps for either game, so this page does not invent them. For the rest of the catalog, browse the arcade games hub.

Buba Jump

From the footage, Buba Jump is a portrait climbing run. The live score sits at the top left, and it rises with height. One missed jump ended the run. GAME OVER shows a TOP SCORE. The goal is to climb as high as you can.

First minute

The recorded run started at a score of 0. About 45 seconds later the score was 86, with a spike monster above. The score then stayed at 86 for about 46 seconds while we tried to get past that enemy, then moved to 94 just after the first minute.

Tips from play

  • The score can sit still for a long time while an enemy is in the way. Ours held at 86 for about 46 seconds.
  • Watch the edges. A cactus sat on the right edge as we reached the 190s.
  • One missed jump is enough to fall off the bottom and end the run.
  • There is a skins shop after a run. We did not use it, and we cut before the PLAY button reappeared.

The rest of the numbers, all read off the screen in one continuous run: 143, then 190 with the cactus at the right edge, then 193, then a miss and a fall, then GAME OVER with TOP SCORE 193. That was our best on a clean run. We did not beat 200.

Car Crash Test

From play, this is a crash-physics sandbox rather than a score chase. We saw a garage menu, then two impacts: one head-on into a yellow wall, and one side scrape, both with smoke. We did not log keys, pedals, or a speedometer, and we are not guessing them here.

First minute

The first minute of this game in our recording was pre-crash: garage and setup. The garage menu showed trophy 0 and an ad offer of "+5,000". That offer is not a score we earned. The first crash came later, well after that opening minute.

Tips from play

  • Do not quote coin or speed numbers unless they are on the frame. We do not have a readable in-crash HUD for either impact.
  • A head-on hit and a side scrape look very different: yellow wall with smoke, versus a side-wall scrape with black smoke.
  • We only have two real crashes from this session, so there is no "best of many runs" number to quote.

Crash 1 went into the yellow wall with smoke. Crash 2 was a side-wall scrape with black smoke. In both cases the crash footage had no coin counter or speedometer visible. This one suits people who want crash physics, not a high score.
